# EcoCrop crop requirements

`ecocrop_2568_plants.csv` holds the ecological requirements of 2,568 plant species from the FAO EcoCrop database (Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, Land and Water Division). `ecocrop_2568_plants_headers.html` is a column guide with Vietnamese translations of the 70 column names.

The data remains the property of FAO and is used here with attribution for a non-commercial hackathon project. Check FAO's current terms of use before reusing it elsewhere.

Known defects are listed in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` §5; the catalogue build cleans them and records what it changed.
