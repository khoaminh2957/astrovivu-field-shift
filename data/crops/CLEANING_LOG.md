Rules, in the order they are tried on a temperature, rainfall or pH window `absMin/optMin/optMax/absMax`:

1. a value outside the physical range (temperature -10..60 °C, rainfall 0..12,000 mm, pH 2..11) is divided by 10 or 100 if that brings it inside;
2. a missing pair of limits is imputed (absolute limits from the median margin of the catalogue: 9 °C below and 6 °C above the optimum for temperature) and the crop is flagged;
3. a value at least three times the limit that should be above it is divided by 10 if that restores the order;
4. absolute and optimal limits typed into each other's column are swapped back;
5. otherwise the absolute window is widened to contain the optimal one; if that fails the window is dropped and the factor is treated as missing.

Latitudes above 90 are altitudes typed into the latitude columns and are dropped. Nothing else is changed.
